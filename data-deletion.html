Data deletion and privacy choices

For Sweet Line: Idle Candy Factory, published by Benjamin Dehant Games.
Updated: 6 October 2026.

Stop optional tracking and ads

Open Privacy from the game's main menu. Select Disable ads and tracking, or disable the relevant measurement option and select Save choices. The game remains playable. This stops future optional gameplay-event collection. Disable Ads and video bonuses to stop new AdMob requests and discard cached ads. When available in the Privacy panel, Google ad privacy options lets you review Google's ad-consent choices. Requests already submitted may still complete.

Delete data on your device

To erase game progress and privacy preferences, use Android Settings → Apps → Sweet Line → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data (labels vary by device). This permanently removes your local progress. Uninstalling also removes app-local data, but device backups or restore settings may restore data later. There is no game account to delete in this version.

Withdrawing game-statistics consent resets Firebase Analytics instance data locally. It does not delete already-sent data from provider systems.

Request deletion of data held by us

  1. Email benjamin.dehant@gmail.com with the subject Sweet Line data deletion.
  2. State whether your request concerns support correspondence, optional game statistics, advertising measurement, AdMob in-game advertising or all applicable data. You can give an approximate date of use and app version to help us investigate.
  3. We will explain any additional information needed to verify the request and locate relevant data. Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents unless a necessary verification method has first been agreed.

We normally respond within one month. Where applicable law permits an extension, we will explain it. We will delete data we control that can be linked to you, unless retention is legally required, and explain any exception or technical limitation. Anonymous aggregate reports may not identify a player. We cannot promise to identify your events from an email address alone, since the game does not collect your email for analytics.

Data processed by Google and Meta

Analytics, measurement and AdMob advertising data sent to providers is subject to their retention settings, roles and deletion procedures. We will assess requests for data under our control and explain available provider procedures where relevant. Account-related data controlled independently by those services can also be managed through their privacy tools:

  • Google data and privacy controls
  • Meta Privacy Center

Deleting your Google or Meta account is not required to stop tracking in Sweet Line.
