Privacy policy

Sweet Line: Idle Candy Factory
Effective date: 5 October 2026
Updated: 12 October 2026
Android package: com.indieben.sweetline

This policy includes the consent and monetization measurement changes prepared for Android version 1.0.6. Earlier versions may not yet offer the separate measurement choices; those integrations remain disabled unless valid permission is available.

1. Publisher and contact

Benjamin Dehant, publishing as Benjamin Dehant Games, is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

2. Playing and age protection

Game progress and age preferences are stored on your device. No game account is required. New players can launch the game and finish the first tutorial without an age or consent popup, ad requests or optional tracking. After that tutorial, a small age gate records an age group locally; we do not request a birth date. This version retains its existing conservative rule: players under 18 use it without ads or optional measurement. This is a product protection, not a statement that every country has the same legal consent age. Closing the age gate without choosing leaves ads and optional measurement disabled. A saved age group is not requested again at every launch.

3. Game statistics — Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase/Google Analytics starts disabled. For eligible adults the resolved Google UMP analytics-storage decision is applied. Explicit denial remains disabled. When that purpose is non-applicable or not configured, a separate explicit statistics choice is offered; missing or unknown decisions are not permission. Earlier refusals remain respected until replaced by an appropriate new explicit choice. Analytics can process an app instance identifier, sessions, device/app information, approximate network-derived location and gameplay events. We use this to understand engagement and improve the game. Before permission only three non-identifying tutorial booleans stay temporarily in RAM; if statistics are subsequently authorized, a present-time tutorial summary is sent, otherwise it is discarded. We do not replay past events or send a name, email, GPS location or complete game save.

4. Advertising measurement — Meta and Google

Google measurement respects applicable Google choices; Analytics advertising ID collection and personalization remain disabled. Meta App Events requires a separate explicit install/campaign measurement choice after the first tutorial. Accepting Google ads does not authorize Meta. Meta is initialized only after eligible permission, with official installation/activation and automatic app-event logging. It may receive device and app information, network information, available advertising identifiers, authorized gameplay events and SDK-provided ad revenue events to measure and improve campaigns. Optional measurement can be refused without preventing gameplay. The Privacy menu can review or withdraw these permissions.

5. In-game ads — Google AdMob

After the age gate, the game updates Google UMP consent information and shows the official Google consent form only when required. Known-age returning players refresh consent information on subsequent launches; a new form appears only if Google requires it. AdMob initialization and all ad requests are gated by Google's canRequestAds result and the game's age protection. A refusal of personalized advertising is not automatically treated as a refusal of all advertising: limited or non-personalized ads may still be permitted by Google's configuration and consent state. Existing explicit in-game ad opt-outs from earlier versions remain respected. Players with an unknown age and players under 18 receive no ad requests. The under-age consent signal is passed to UMP for the protected age group.

Placements are ads between completed levels and optional videos for a bonus. Google's Mobile Ads SDK can process IP address (approximate location), app/ad interactions, device and other identifiers including the advertising ID when available, and diagnostic/performance information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. All requests retain non-personalized publisher treatment and G-rated creative filtering. Non-personalized ads may still process data. We do not request GPS location.

The main-menu Privacy link manages optional measurement and opens Google's official privacy-options form when available/required without repeating the age gate. Before age handling or for protected players it opens this privacy information. Development ad units are test units and do not earn revenue. Advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted in Android settings.

6. Other information

If you email us for support or deletion, we process your email address, message and any information you choose to provide to handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information. This version does not implement account registration, real in-app purchases, camera, microphone, contacts access or precise location collection.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Optional statistics require an explicit applicable Google analytics grant or, for a non-applicable/unconfigured purpose, explicit local statistics permission. Advertising processing follows official Google choices, SDK eligibility and applicable law. Meta requires its own permission. We use authorized impression counts and SDK-provided impression revenues, formats, networks, currencies and precision to measure monetization, retention and advertising performance. Refusals and under-age protection remain respected. Support and rights requests are handled on our legitimate interest in assisting players and applicable legal obligations. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

8. Recipients and international processing

Authorized measurement data is sent to Google/Firebase and, only with valid Meta authorization, Meta. Where Google permits ad requests, ad-related data is processed by Google AdMob for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We access reporting through their services. We do not sell your personal data for money. Providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area; their privacy documentation describes applicable transfer safeguards.

9. Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until you erase the app's data or remove it, subject to device backup and restore settings. Measurement and advertising data already sent is retained according to the configured provider retention settings and their policies; withdrawing consent does not automatically delete it. Aggregated reports may be retained separately from identifiable event data. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us for information about retention applicable to your request or to request deletion.

10. Changing choices and deleting data

Open Privacy from the game's main menu to review measurement choices and Google's privacy options when available. This does not restart the age gate. Changed choices are applied before new ads or optional events are permitted; withdrawing statistics permission disables collection and resets local Firebase Analytics instance data. If Google still permits limited ads after a refusal of personalization, those ads may continue. Earlier explicit ad opt-outs remain respected. Already submitted provider requests cannot be recalled, and withdrawal does not automatically erase data held by providers. Local erasure and deletion-request instructions are on our data deletion page.

11. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing where applicable, and withdraw consent. Contact us at the address above. We may need limited information to verify and locate the relevant data; aggregated reports may not identify you. We normally respond within one month, subject to extensions permitted by law, and will explain any limitation. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

12. Players under 18

This version retains its existing conservative protection for players who select Under 18: no optional analytics, campaign measurement or in-game ad requests. The age group is stored locally, and the under-age consent signal is passed to Google UMP. We do not ask for a name, email or birth date to play. The threshold is a game policy, not a universal legal age of digital consent. If you believe data was collected contrary to these protections, contact us.

13. Security and updates

Provider SDKs use encrypted network connections. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. This policy describes the current Android implementation. Material changes to collection or purposes will be reflected here and, where required, in renewed in-game consent.
