Privacy policy

Sweet Line: Idle Candy Factory
Effective date: 5 October 2026
Android package: com.indieben.sweetline

1. Publisher and contact

Benjamin Dehant, publishing as Benjamin Dehant Games, is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

2. Playing without tracking

Game progress and privacy preferences are stored on your device. No game account is required. You can play without optional analytics or advertising measurement. In this version, optional measurement is enabled only when you declare that you are 18 or older and affirmatively select the corresponding option. The age choice is stored locally; we do not request your date of birth.

3. Optional game statistics — Google Firebase Analytics

If you enable Game statistics, Firebase/Google Analytics processes app launches and sessions, an app instance identifier, device and app information, and approximate location derived from network information. It receives gameplay events such as tutorial progress, screens visited, recipe unlocks, level outcomes, factory upgrades, missions and offline rewards. We use these statistics to understand engagement, retention and game progression and improve the game. We do not deliberately send your name, email, precise location or the complete game save as analytics parameters.

4. Optional advertising measurement — Meta and Google

If you enable Advertising measurement, Meta App Events receives app activity and gameplay events, device and app information, network information including IP address and, where available, the Android advertising identifier. This helps measure the performance of advertisements promoting the game. Meta may associate information with its own identifiers under its policies.

If both statistics and advertising measurement are enabled, Google Analytics consent for advertising storage and advertising user data is granted. Google advertising ID collection and Google advertising personalization remain disabled in this version. These measurement choices do not themselves display an advertisement or make a purchase.

5. Other information

If you email us for support or deletion, we process your email address, message and any information you choose to provide to handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information. This version does not implement account registration, real in-app purchases, camera, microphone, contacts access or precise location collection.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Optional analytics and advertising measurement rely on your consent. You can withdraw it in the game. Handling support and rights requests is based on our legitimate interest in assisting players and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

7. Recipients and international processing

Optional measurement data is sent to Google/Firebase and Meta when the corresponding choice is enabled. We access reporting through their services. We do not sell your personal data for money. These providers may process information in countries outside your own, including outside the European Economic Area; applicable transfer mechanisms and safeguards are described in their privacy documentation.

8. Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until you erase the app's data or remove it, subject to device backup and restore settings. Measurement data already sent is retained according to the configured provider retention settings and their policies; withdrawing consent does not automatically delete it. Aggregated reports may be retained separately from identifiable event data. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us for information about retention applicable to your request or to request deletion.

9. Changing choices and deleting data

Open Privacy choices in the game and choose Play without tracking, or turn off the relevant option and save. This stops future optional gameplay-event collection through the game's measurement integration. Withdrawing statistics consent also resets local Firebase Analytics instance data. It does not automatically erase provider-held data or recall requests already submitted. Instructions for local data and requests are available on our data deletion page.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing where applicable, and withdraw consent. Contact us at the address above. We may need limited information to verify and locate the relevant data; aggregated reports may not identify you. We normally respond within one month, subject to extensions permitted by law, and will explain any limitation. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

11. Players under 18

Players who select an age group under 18 use this version without optional analytics or advertising measurement. We do not ask them to supply a name, email or birth date to play. If you believe information was collected contrary to these choices, contact us.

12. Security and updates

Provider SDKs use encrypted network connections. No transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. This policy describes the current Android implementation. Material changes to collection or purposes will be reflected here and, where required, in renewed in-game consent.
